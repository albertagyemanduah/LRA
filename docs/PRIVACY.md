# Privacy Policy

**Techiman North Land Registry** is operated by the Techiman North District Assembly (TeNDA), Ghana.

## Data Collected

- **Land parcel data**: owner name, contact numbers, plot details, allocation/registration dates.
- **User account data**: full name, Ghana Card number, email address, phone number, role.
- **Audit logs**: action, timestamp, IP address of the performing officer.
- **Verification logs**: search queries, IP address, timestamp (no user identity for public searches).

## Purpose of Data Collection

- Administration and digitization of land records for the Techiman North District.
- Notification of applicants via SMS and email about their land application status.
- Internal audit and accountability trail for District Assembly officers.

## Data Storage

All data is stored on servers within the Hostinger hosting infrastructure. The database is encrypted at rest using AES-256 (PocketBase encryption key). Uploaded files are stored in PocketBase managed storage.

## Data Retention

- Land parcel records are retained indefinitely as legal records of the District Assembly.
- Audit logs are retained for a minimum of 7 years.
- Deleted (soft-deleted) records are retained until permanently purged by an administrator.
- OTP sessions expire after 8 hours and are not retained.

## Data Sharing

Land parcel data is not shared with third parties. SMS notifications are sent via Arkesel (a Ghanaian SMS gateway) — only the recipient phone number and message text are transmitted.

## User Rights

District Assembly officers may request correction of their personal account data by contacting a system administrator. Land parcel data may only be amended through the official Amendment workflow.
